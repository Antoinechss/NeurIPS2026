# Timeline

The competition runs as a **single phase**. The public leaderboard column
gives live feedback, while the hidden private column decides the final
ranking. Up to **5 submissions per day**, each trained within **1 hour** on
a GPU.

- **Start:** 29 October 2026
- **End:** 1 February 2027

The final ranking is taken from the private (hidden) score at the close of the
phase.
